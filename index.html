The planet is warming. We can still steer it.

Sustainable Development Goal 13 calls for urgent action against climate change and its impacts. In the 2015 Paris Agreement, countries agreed to hold warming well below 2°C and to pursue 1.5°C.

What can we do?

Climate action works at every level. Filter by who acts.

🥗 Eat more plants

Plant-rich meals usually have a smaller carbon footprint.

🚲 Move smarter

Walk, cycle or ride public transport for everyday trips.

🌳 Plant and protect

Community tree planting cools streets and stores carbon.

🔧 Repair and share

Repair cafés and swap events keep products in use longer.

☀️ Clean power

Expand solar and wind, and phase out coal.

🏞️ Protect nature

Safeguard forests and wetlands that absorb carbon.
